This supplement does not restate project-level rules; it records only what the feature adds, what it must not change, and the decisions left open.

Reference docs: `docs/medication-scheduler-implementation-plan.md` (the shipped scheduler architecture, escalation algorithm, safety invariants, observability contract), `requirements.md` FR-026..FR-030, `requirements-dementia-supplement.md` FR-D01..FR-D04e. ## Feature Purpose & Scope

Purpose: revamp medication schedules and reminders in the existing iOS app so the feature is fit for forgetful elderly users tracking what they have taken and what they have not. The owner's seven scope points:

1. **Entry model becomes quantity + time-of-day (morning/afternoon/evening) + before/after meal** — not raw clock times.
2. **Each medication carries a brief purpose description.**
3. **Camera scan of packaging/label fills the name and schedules**, with manual entry as before.
4. **The elder acknowledges intake in natural voice** ("I have taken X for morning before meal") and intake activities are recorded against daily schedules.
5. **A photo of the medication serves as intake evidence** — easier than dictating names when several medications share one schedule slot.
6. **Caregivers are always notified of significant medication events** via the caregiver app, calendar events, and other channels.
7. **Core requirement: fit for forgetful elderly users.**

Primary user: the forgetful elderly person (60+, many with cognitive challenges, non-English first language) using the app day-to-day. Secondary: the adult-child caregiver who configures schedules and watches for problems.

Observable success: the elder can always answer "what have I taken and what have I not" for each morning/afternoon/evening slot with near-zero friction — acknowledging intake by voice or by photographing the medication; every intake is recorded against the day's schedule; caregivers are reliably notified of every significant medication event.

In scope:

- The slot-based entry model (quantity + slot + before/after meal) with a deterministic, family-configurable slot→clock-time mapping that keeps the existing exact-time scheduling machinery binding.
- Entry-model migration, and the editor/settings surfaces that produce `MedicationEntry` payloads.
- Purpose-at-entry (owner point 2), building on the shipped `purpose` field, chips, and lookup — not a new mechanism.
- Scan-to-fill as an additive path beside manual entry (the shipped label OCR/scanner integrated, not rebuilt).
- The voice intake-acknowledgement path ("I have taken X for morning before meal") and its recording against the day's schedule, through the existing acknowledgement flow.
- Photo-as-evidence for intake, in the slot-sharing use case.
- Caregiver notification of significant medication events across the existing channels and taxonomy.
- Elderly-fit UX for all of the above (voice-first, 44pt+, 18pt+, plain language).

Out of scope (must not change):

- **Emergency-call and health-monitoring modules** — untouched.
- **Android** — deferred to v2; not re-opened.
- **No weakening of any existing medication safety invariant; no removal of the existing dementia machinery** (confirmation challenge, double-dose detection, photo verification).
- Existing shipped machinery is integrated, not rebuilt: purpose chips/lookup, label OCR/scanner, `PhotoVerifier`, `ConfirmationChallenge`/`DoubleDoseDetector`, `FamilyNotifier` + caregiver event notifications, `MedicationAdherenceLog`.
- **No new cloud egress for medication content.** Label/photo capture processing stays on-device; the recorded cloud exceptions (root Open Decisions 12, voice transcription; 13, OCR'd-text translation) do NOT apply to this feature. (Honest note: the shipped purpose lookup does send the medicine NAME through the existing search stack under its existing disclosure — `MedicationPurposeLookup.swift` — that is the search stack's egress, not a new one this feature introduces.)
- The Devanagari substring/grapheme-cluster behavior (regression-pinned — do not "fix" it).

## Entry Model & Slot-to-Clock Mapping Contract

Verified ground truth: today the entry surface is raw clock times (`MedicationEntry.scheduleTimes`, hour/minute `DateComponents`, `Models.swift:31`), and everything safety-shaped downstream binds to one concrete `scheduledAt`: `createPendingReminders` resolves the next occurrence (`MedicationScheduler.swift:707`, `:747`), the `EscalationEngine` derives ack deadline, re-fire schedule, escalation deadline and the missed-dose verdict from `scheduledTime` (`EscalationEngine.swift:29–75`), and the reminder is persisted before the platform alarm is armed.

Binding properties:

1. The entry surface becomes quantity + slot (morning/afternoon/evening) + before/after meal. The persisted scheduling layer must still resolve every entry to exact clock times: no reminder may be armed without a determinate hour/minute, and the slot→clock mapping must be **deterministic and total** over the creatable entry space. This is the brief's safety_delta (b): map deterministically, or ack windows / re-fire / escalation stop binding.
2. The mapping is expected to be family-configurable per user, with sane defaults (scaffold clarification; the default values are Open Decision 1). It must preserve exact-time scheduling — Architecture Constraint 4 (background reliability) is unchanged.
3. `quantity` is new. `doseDescription` remains the rendered dose line (fired-dose screen, briefing, and the editor's scanned strength all read it — `MedicationEditorDraft.storedStrength`, `AppCoordinator.addMedication`). Quantity's representation and its relationship to `doseDescription` and `DoubleDoseDetector` are Open Decision 2 — verified: the detector is keyed on `medicationEntryId` + `windowHours` and takes no dose/quantity input (`DoubleDoseDetector.swift:21`).
4. Purpose-at-entry (owner point 2) builds on the shipped field and machinery (`MedicationEntry.purpose` :50; `MedicationPurpose` chips + `MedicationVoiceVocabulary`; `MedicationPurposeLookupService`). Whether purpose becomes required-at-entry is design-level; pre-purpose payloads must keep loading either way.
5. **Migration rule (binding).** New model fields optional with custom-decoder defaults; `Models.swift init(from:)` (:114–131) is the precedent; pre-existing payloads (no quantity/slot fields, raw `scheduleTimes`, no purpose, no visualAids) must keep loading, and legacy `scheduleTimes` remain readable and representable. Never lose a household's schedule to a missing key.
6. The shipping editor constructs `frequency: .daily` at every construction site; the enum's `.weekly`/`.custom` cases stay decodable. How (or whether) the slot model represents non-daily entries is part of Open Decision 5.
7. The editor draft rules are bound, not to be relaxed: `MedicationEditorDraft` — text pre-fill into blank fields only, the label's stated schedule wins, an empty read changes nothing, `storedTimes` is `[.hour, .minute]` only, `canSave` requires a name (:69, :143, :149). The revamp extends this draft type; it does not loosen these rules.

## Intake Acknowledgement & Recording Contract

Verified ground truth: today's voice ack is name-agnostic — the ack vocabulary (`CommandRouter.medicationAckPhrases` :2434, tokens :2443, `isExplicitMedicationAcknowledgement` :2460, refusal-first guard :2425) matches phrases; `handleMedicationAcknowledgement` (:5381) picks `coordinator.oldestPendingReminderEntryId()` (:12929) and issues the FR-D01 challenge; the yes/no answer routes `handleMedicationConfirmation` (:12647) → `acknowledgeWithConfirmation` → `ConfirmationChallenge` → `DoubleDoseDetector` → record (`MedicationScheduler.swift:241–283`, `:499–549`).

Binding properties:

1. The elder acknowledges in natural voice naming the medication and the slot ("I have taken X for morning before meal"), and the utterance resolves deterministically to the correct reminder + entry. Today's oldest-pending-reminder selection does not suffice when several medications share a slot — which is owner point 5's own stated motivation — so resolution is by what the elder said, not by firing order.
2. The existing safety sequence keeps its order and is never bypassed: acknowledgement → FR-D01 `ConfirmationChallenge` → FR-D03 `DoubleDoseDetector` → record. No new acknowledgement path may record a dose that skipped the challenge (the issue-on-first-call behaviour at `MedicationScheduler.swift:241–263` exists precisely so it cannot).
3. Recording: every intake is recorded against the day's schedule — a `MedicationAdherenceLog` entry carrying the reminder's `scheduledAt` and the `acknowledgedAt` (the FR-D04 caregiver timeline reads this).
4. Acknowledgement is persisted immediately (root safety constraint; `finalizeAcknowledgement` writes the adherence log before cleanup, :453–497), and a reminder the app was killed before acknowledging re-fires on next launch within the unexpired escalation window (FR-029; `restoreState`/`rearmAllPendingReminders` :611/:646).
5. Ambiguity is conservative and deterministic: when several medications share the slot or the utterance is ambiguous, the system disambiguates ("did you mean X / X or Y" — the owner copy rule) rather than guessing; it must never record the wrong medication.
6. Denials stay refusal-first: the `medicationDenialPhrases` guard runs before the ack list everywhere the vocabulary is used (:2425–2467) — a denial must never fall through to an ack.
7. The schedule/recording path remains LLM-independent: no `LlamaInferenceEngine` dependency in the medication scheduler compilation unit.

## Scan-to-Fill Contract

Verified ground truth — the machinery ships: `MedicationLabelOCR.swift` (candidate :41, pure parser :86, injectable `MedicationLabelRecognizing` :489, Vision recognizer :504 — on-device, "no network client, no API key and no URL anywhere in this file"); `MedicationLabelScanner.swift` (scan result :28, scanner :44, spoken outcomes, metadata-free events); `MedicationEditorDraft.applyScan` (:69).

Binding properties:

1. Scan is additive; manual entry remains first-class ("with manual entry as before", owner point 3). A household that never scans keeps today's exact path.
2. **OCR'd values require human confirmation before they bind — never silent auto-apply into the safety-critical schedule** (safety_delta (a)). This holds today by construction: the parser's output is a candidate, nothing writes a store, and the entry is only created by the family's save (`SettingsView.swift:4674` → `AppCoordinator.addMedication` :11634); the OCR file's header states the invariant ("Advisory, never authoritative… an OCR misread of '1-0-1' as '1-0-7' must cost a correction, never a dose"). Any new scan entry point must route through the same review-and-save gate.
3. The extraction surface is bounded by the conservative parser: name, strength, schedule times (time words; the `1-0-1` day pattern), frequency guess — and it refuses to guess ("a missed field costs one typed word; a wrong schedule costs a dose"). Purpose is not scannable from a label (it is not printed; the lookup is the separate, gated path). What the scan fills, the confirm step, and partial-fill behaviour are Open Decision 4.
4. Partial and failed reads are honest and non-destructive: text fills blank fields only, the label's stated schedule replaces the form's, an empty read changes nothing and is spoken ("could not read it"), and the photo is never discarded (`.scanned` carries the image even when the text did not come — `MedicationLabelScanner.finish` :160).
5. Processing stays on-device: no medication photos or label content to cloud (constraint (b)). Root OD-12/OD-13 do not apply.
6. Scan outcomes stay observable and metadata-free (`medication_label_scan` events carry no image, no recognized text).

## Photo-as-Evidence Contract

Note the distinction this contract must hold: owner point 5 is a photo OF the medication as intake evidence; the shipped FR-D04a machinery is an empty-hand presence check attached after acknowledgement, with E2E delivery and per-medication/per-time-of-day caregiver config.

Binding properties:

1. A photo of the medication serves as intake evidence, **augmenting and never replacing the acknowledgement + confirmation chain** (safety_delta (c)). Recording still happens through the acknowledgement path; evidence attaches to a record, it does not create one.
2. The driving use case is the shared slot: several medications share one schedule slot, and photographing is easier than dictating names — so the evidence path must resolve/record against the correct schedule slot deterministically (same requirement as the voice ack, same conservative disambiguation).
3. On-device only: no medication photos to cloud; root OD-12/OD-13 do not apply.
4. Verified state of the shipped dementia machinery: `PhotoVerifier` (FR-D04a-e: 200 KB cap, E2E delivery, delete-within-1-hour, graceful camera fallback) **exists as components but is not wired end-to-end in the app** — no call sites outside its own file, `photoVerificationEnabled` is `false` at every entry-construction site (`AppCoordinator.swift:11667`, `:12952`), there are no `PhotoVerifier` tests, the image compression is a documented placeholder (:99–111), and the delivery channel is the stubbed path below. The feature must not assume FR-D04b delivery works today.
5. Where evidence leaves the device it follows the FR-D04b discipline (compression cap, E2E encryption, on-device retention until delivered then deletion within 1 hour, never stored on the relay, never used for any other purpose) — and delivery failure is never silent.
6. Whether the new photo-as-evidence reuses `PhotoVerifier`'s E2E path, ships an on-device-only variant, or extends the per-medication dementia mechanism is Open Decision 8. Removal of the dementia mechanism is forbidden either way.

## Caregiver Notification Contract

Verified ground truth: `FamilyAlertType` (:214) is the PII-free wire taxonomy (`emergencyCall`, `missedMedication`, `healthMonitoringInterrupted`, `configurationUpdateApplied`, `possibleDoubleDose`, `inactivityAlert`, `eventReminder`); `missedMedication` carries "entry_id_hash only, no medication name". The envelope is `{v:1, alert_type, timestamp}` (`FamilyNotifier.buildPayload` :153); `FamilyAlertContext` (:58) carries the human-readable title IN MEMORY ONLY, and its comment states the future relay "MUST re-litigate title-on-wire explicitly". Triggers ship: missed dose → `notifyAll(.missedMedication)` (:551–586); double-dose attempt → `.possibleDoubleDose` (:532); first reminder fire → `.eventReminder` gated by `CaregiverNotifySettings` (defaults OFF, :66–73; read at fire time; `notifyCaregiversOfFirstFire` :351); calendar events → `CaregiverEventFireHandler` :34. Delivery transport is a stub (`APNsProvider` prints + returns true, :217; the E2E channel plan is unimplemented and defers photo delivery).

Binding properties:

1. Caregivers are always notified of significant medication events (owner point 6). What counts as "significant" — and whether "always" changes the existing opt-in semantics (`CaregiverNotifySettings` defaults OFF; `missedMedication`/`possibleDoubleDose` already fire unconditionally while `eventReminder` is gated) — is Open Decision 3.
2. The wire stays PII-free: significant-event notifications extend the existing `FamilyAlertType` taxonomy; no new PII on the wire (safety_delta (d)). Putting medication names or titles on the wire is an owner-level decision per the `FamilyAlertContext` rule — if the design wants caregiver-useful content, that decision is made explicitly, not smuggled in.
3. The existing channels keep working as paths: caregiver app push (APNs path), calendar events (consent-gated `CalendarShareService`, fed by `MedicationScheduler.onScheduleChanged` :58 on every schedule write), and the text channels `NotifyChannel` derives from each contact's calling preference. The mechanism for the "calendar events" channel is Open Decision 7.
4. Notification decisions resolve at fire time, never stored per event (`CaregiverNotifySettings` contract) — the revamp keeps that shape.
5. Per-contact delivery results are honest: `NotificationResult` carries success/errorCode/channel; partial delivery is acceptable but must be reported as itself, never as success.
6. **Honest delivery status (verified):** the app-side triggers fire today, but the push transport is a stub and the E2E channel is planned work (`docs/family-notifier-e2e-implementation-plan.md`) — "always notified" cannot be delivered end-to-end until that transport lands. This feature must not claim a delivery that did not happen (no silent stubs, Agent Principles); complying here means honest status and, if the owner wants real delivery in this feature, adopting the transport as scope.
7. Medication alarms are not claimed by `CaregiverEventFireHandler` (its comment: "medication alarms carry no `type`") — the medication notify path is `notifyCaregiversOfFirstFire` + `recordMissedDose` + the double-dose alert. New significant-event triggers belong on that path, not smuggled through the event-fire handler.

## Safety & Compliance Delta

No new compliance regime — the feature operates entirely inside the existing safety-critical medication profile (App Store/Play health policies; root constitution safety constraints). Safety-relevant NEW surfaces the design must treat explicitly under the existing invariants, per the brief's safety_delta:

- **(a) Scan-to-fill writes into a safety-critical schedule.** OCR'd name/schedule values require human confirmation before they bind, never silent auto-apply. Mitigated by construction today (Scan-to-Fill 2); binding on every new scan entry point.
- **(b) Slot→clock mapping.** The quantity + time-of-day entry model must map deterministically to exact reminder clock times, or ack windows / re-fire / escalation stop binding. The highest-risk surface of the entry revamp: everything safety-shaped downstream of a reminder reads one `Date`.
- **(c) Photo-as-intake-evidence augments and never replaces the acknowledgement + confirmation chain.**
- **(d) Caregiver "always notified" extends the existing PII-free `FamilyAlertType` taxonomy with no new PII on the wire.**

Additional verified risk notes the gates should see:

- **`PhotoVerifier` is unwired** (Photo-as-Evidence 4). Treating FR-D04a-e as a working dependency is a false premise; where the design builds on it, wiring it is part of that work.
- **The push transport is a stub** (Caregiver Notification 6). Delivery-dependent claims are aspirational until the E2E channel lands.
- **Mic-continuity invariant around acks.** The ack fast lane (`Services/Voice/AckFastLane.swift`, `AckFastLanePlayer`; suite `ElderlyAssistantTests/Services/Voice/AckFastLaneTests.swift`) plays acks so the mic never closes ([ACK-MIC-CONTINUITY], header comment). The new ack-with-slot utterance enters the same turn pipeline and must not reintroduce a gap between acknowledgement and listening.
- **Correction interactions.** The unrecognised path's correction rescue ("did you mean X / X or Y") coexists with the ack vocabulary; new utterance shapes must neither shadow nor be shadowed by it.
- **T2/test rigor.** The workflow file runs full-feature with `risk_tier: T1` defaults while its header declares "Safety delta: yes — review T2 gates". Project Standards bind regardless (confidence 0.85 on implementation, paired review on implementation tasks, max rework 5, 100% unit coverage + integration tests on medication paths); the dementia supplement's elevated 0.90 threshold applies to decisions touching the FR-D01/FR-D03/FR-D04a-b machinery this feature interfaces (`requirements-dementia-supplement.md` §4.4). Raising the run's risk tier / adding the T2 gate is an operator action on the workflow file; this supplement is the delta those gates review against.

## Feature Constraints

1. **Root constraints bind unchanged.** 100% unit coverage + integration tests on safety-critical medication paths; reminder acknowledgement persisted and re-fired on next launch (FR-029); persistence-before-alarm; LLM independence (the medication scheduler compilation unit must not depend on `LlamaInferenceEngine`); PII-free observability (`entry_id_hash` only, `LogSanitiser`); on-device AI. Plus the brief's feature-specific constraints: (a) migration without loss; (b) on-device label/photo processing; (c) elderly-fit UX; (d) reliable background firing with the slot→clock mapping preserving exact-time scheduling; (e) manual entry first-class; (f) existing shipped machinery integrated, not rebuilt.
2. **Migration rule (house rule).** New model fields optional with custom-decoder defaults; pre-existing `MedicationEntry` payloads (no purpose, no visualAids, raw `scheduleTimes`) must keep loading — `Models.swift init(from:)` is the precedent; never lose a household's schedule to a missing key. Legacy `scheduleTimes` remain readable and representable.
3. **Delivery rule (house rule).** Task work runs ONLY in the dedicated worktree (sibling of the main checkout, branch `worktree-<slug>` / `feat/<slug>`), integrated via PR against master; never in the main checkout.
4. **Test gate.** Scoped/focused XCTest suites are the honest gate (the full bundle has a known pre-existing failure baseline). Medication suites: `MedicationSchedulerTests`, `EscalationEngineTests`, `ConfirmationChallengeTests`, `DoubleDoseDetectorTests`, `MedicationEditorDraftTests`, `MedicationLabelOCRTests`, `MedicationLabelScannerTests`, `MedicationPurposeTests`, `MedicationPurposeLookupTests`, `MedicationEntryVisualAidTests`; notifier suites: `FamilyNotifierTests`, `CaregiverEventFireHandlerTests`, `CaregiverNotifySettingsTests`, `NotifyChannelTests`. Persistence-before-alarm ordering and process-kill re-arm (`scheduleAll()` on launch) integration tests are required (per `docs/medication-scheduler-implementation-plan.md` §Testing strategy).
5. **Observability.** PII-free events (`entry_id_hash` only, `IdHashing.shortHash`, `LogSanitiser`); the release-build log-surface gate (`ios/tools/check-release-log-safety.sh`, wired into `ios/build.sh`) must cover the new paths.
6. **Localization.** All new user-visible strings ne/en in `ios/ElderlyAssistant/Resources/Localizable.xcstrings`; spoken times ONLY via the `SpokenTime` formatter (mandatory project standard — e.g. `todayMedicationSummaryLines` reads through it); the Devanagari substring/grapheme-cluster behavior is regression-pinned — do not "fix" it.
7. **On-device.** Medication label/photo processing stays on-device; the recorded cloud exceptions (root OD-12, OD-13) do not apply to this feature.
8. **Elderly-fit UX (house rule).** Voice-first acknowledgement; 44pt+ targets; 18pt+ body text; plain-language copy consistent with the owner's copy rules (the correction rule: "did you mean X / X or Y" ONLY — no leads, no numbered lists, ever).
9. **No silent stubs; honest degradation.** Every deferred capability (unwired photo verification, stub transport) is surfaced as itself; the feature must not present an unverified write or an undelivered notification as success.
10. **Explicitly out of scope (must not change).** Emergency-call and health-monitoring modules; Android (v2); the dementia machinery (removal forbidden, extension is the mode); the `FamilyAlertType` PII-free wire discipline absent an explicit owner decision.
