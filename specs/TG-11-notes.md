## The gate thresholds

Every dimension carries a measured gate, and every gate carries a named fixture, a revision tag and a failing fixture that proves it trips in isolation (T-067, the T-038 sweep pattern).

- **Order-invariance: X = 3 percentage points.** Closed-intent accuracy on permuted rows must be within 3 points of their parents *in the same run*, paired. Justified by the project's existing equivalence band `max_gap_vs_gemini: 0.03` (`config.yaml:63`), by the paired resolution arithmetic (≈2.2-point CI half-width at 800 pairs, π_d≈0.10; at 400 pairs the half-width ≈3.2 points is wider than the gate), and by the width of the real failure (a content-anchored model moves 0–2 points, a position-anchored one collapses 10–30). Three anti-gaming clauses ride with it: emergency recall on the fixture must stay 1.00, the fixture abstention rate must not exceed the parents' by more than 0.03, and span F1 must not fall more than 0.03 below the parents'. Tier B operators (`O2`, `O4`) are reported at an expected ≤6 points and fail only above 10 — they are measured but not trained, and the design says why (§4.2, §6).
- **Dialect robustness: D = 5 percentage points.** Per-slice closed-intent accuracy must be within 5 points of the matched standard twin, paired, with ≥300 twin pairs per claimed slice (≈3.6-point CI half-width). Deliberately wider than the order gate because dialect changes the *words*, not their order. The absolute clauses carry the safety weight: per slice, side-effect precision ≥ 0.97 and emergency recall = 1.00 (the existing hard gates, applied at slice granularity). **No absolute accuracy floor was invented** — the gate proves non-collapse on the slices actually authored, and the claim stays per-slice and evidence-bound.

- **Additive noise (SNR ladder): Δ ≤ 3 points at 15 dB, ≤ 5 points at 10 and 5 dB, fail-safe at 3 dB.** Levels {15, 10, 5, 3} dB, taken from the **existing** augmentation band (`tools/train/src/dataset.py:42-43`, white noise uniform in [3, 15] dB, `--no-aug` at `train_finetune.py:53-55`, training-only) rather than invented — nothing below 3 dB is generated, because gating outside the band the project trains for measures the fixture rather than the model. The tighter clause attaches to the level whose interval can resolve it (≥800 pairs at 15 dB, ≥300 elsewhere). At the 3 dB floor the requirement is **fail-safe** — the model degrades toward re-prompting, with confident errors not exceeding abstentions — because a hard accuracy number at the loudest level would either be invented or be trivially met. `side_effect_precision ≥ 0.97` and `emergency_recall == 1.00` hold at every level, and a ladder that *improves* with more noise beyond a 3-point tolerance is refused as a fixture or scoring defect rather than reported as a robustness result.
- **Clipped tail: Δ ≤ 5 points.** The same 5-point band as dialect, for the same reason (material is removed, not reordered), with the frozen-material rule enforced in the **generator**: polarity/negation markers, emergency rows and the `music`/`suggest_video` verb pair cannot be elided, so no fixture row exists in which elision manufactures a label error for the model to be measured against. Tail-critical families are printed with their own delta rather than folded into the aggregate.
- **Reduced articulation (slur proxy): Δ ≤ 5 points at the moderate cell, fail-safe at the severe cell.** Cells are the declared rate/tempo/quality/noise grid (moderate: rate within ±20% of nominal, mild quality perturbation, SNR 10 dB; severe: rate ±40%, stronger perturbation, SNR 5 dB), with the absolute clauses at every cell. Named a **proxy** everywhere it appears — never a dysarthria or slurred-speech result.
- **Accent: not a threshold — a declared gap (GAP-3).** The voice bank holds one voice and it is a Hindi voice (`config.yaml:23`), so the multi-voice gate is written into config as an unwired threshold and rendered as GAP-3 with the voices it needs. A gate that cannot be measured is never rendered green.

## Decisions made during implementation

- **Task IDs are T-061–T-068.** Verified against the tree before writing: T-060 (`TG-10-loop-end-to-end-fixture.md`) is the highest existing ID and no `T-06x` file existed above it, so no renumbering was needed and no ID was reused.
- **The brief's "v4 model" does not exist at this base.** `master` @ `41daeb6` pins the T-036 **v3** 8k-topup export (`ModelCatalog.intentEncoderSpike` entry, run `t036-full-0.1.0-internal-noised6b-topup-*`, checkpoint `0bd6bafb`, int8 zip 109,079,441 B). No v4 appears anywhere in the history. T-061 and the design therefore name the artifact by **digest prefix + run id + catalog entry** and never by a version word, so a v4 landing mid-flight changes which digest is measured and nothing else. Recorded as design OQ-6.
- **The brief's own example is half-satisfied by the corpus, and the design says so.** "भोलिको मौसम कस्तो छ" maps onto the existing `query` action (there is no `weather` label and the closed-taxonomy rule at `annotation_rules.yaml:9-11` forbids adding one), but **`query` rows supervise no spans today** — every `QUERY_*` shape in `golden_corpus_batches.py` declares `{}` slots and the hand rows `gc-query-001…016` have empty span lists, and the same policy explicitly excludes embedded method mentions (`annotation_rules.yaml:291-294`). So "slot भोलि" is a **span-policy amendment**, not a robustness fix: it is recorded as design OQ-1 and owned by T-063, with its consequences stated (the harness's slot-F1 population changes; no pinned row is re-annotated). It was not smuggled in as augmentation.
- **`dialect`/`style` are orthogonal row metadata, not new registers.** `register` selects the mixture bucket (`build_dataset.py:77-82`) and the 60/25/15 targets hang off it (`annotation_rules.yaml:207-216`); adding values there would change the mixture arithmetic for the whole corpus. The axes change no model label, so adopting them forces no retrain.
- **The noise pass is not accent modelling, and nothing in the deliverables claims it is.** The shipped voice is a **Hindi** voice (`hi_IN-pratham-medium`) chosen as nearest-to-Nepali and `synthesize` passes only `--model`/`--output_file` (`stt_noise.py:30-48`), so the round trip produces an STT error profile, not regional accent. T-066 adds deterministic `(voice, length_scale, noise_scale, noise_w)` tuples and labels the output honestly; the design says the limit out loud (§5.5) rather than dressing it up.
- **Permutation runs upstream of the noise pass.** Order variance then lands inside the 60% `stt_noised` bucket at no extra round-trip cost, and the consequence — a two-hop parent chain `noised → permuted → original` — is stated in both the design (§4.5) and T-066, with the transitive guard required to follow it.
- **The parent-key hazard is the group's own creation and is treated as such.** A permuted or dialect row is not normalize-equal to its parent, so the exactness leak guard cannot see it, and a permuted row through the noise pass is invisible twice. The build already measures the same blind spot for the existing noised rows — **118 rows whose `clean_utterance` parent is a golden utterance (22 keys)** (`build_encoder_dataset.py:609-611`). Controls: a transitive `parent_leak` counter kept separate from the exactness counter, all new fixture paths registered in one place (`pipeline_guards.GOLDEN_CORPUS` as a collection), `--waive-leak` explicitly not waiving it, and T-068 constructing one-hop and two-hop adversarial rows that must be refused.
- **No floor was lowered and no existing gate value was touched.** `corpus_floor: 8000`, `hard_floor_stt_noised: 0.55` and `per_action_floor` are cited as constraints, not re-normalised; the five new gates are additive; `max_gap_vs_gemini: 0.03` is cited as precedent, never re-tuned. If order/dialect supply cannot clear a floor, that is a finding to report.
- **Risk numbering** in `plan.md` continues the existing list (items 32–39) and the TG-11 paragraph in "Security blockers" follows the existing "No new security BLOCKERs are introduced by X" pattern. Both were appended, not inserted mid-list.
- **The design declines to invent a dialect atlas.** The five-value inventory (Eastern/Central/Western/Terai + standard) is written as a working hypothesis and handed to T-062 for native-speaker validation, including the two questions an engineer should not answer alone: whether Far-Western is distinct enough to be its own slice, and whether the Terai belt is one slice or several. No per-dialect verb paradigm is asserted from memory.

- **The evidence-first standard was applied to the group's own structure.** Every robustness claim in the design maps to a gate with (a) a named corpus revision, (b) a threshold with its justification, (c) a failing fixture that proves the gate trips **in isolation** — the T-038 sweep discipline, `eval/fixtures/` + `run_fixture_sweep.sh:5-7`, "exit 1 with EXACTLY the one gate it targets" — and (d) the exact rows (the deterministic row-selection rule plus the row ids in the fixture manifest). The pack that holds this together is an artifact with a check ([T-069](../.ai-sdd/outputs/plan-tasks/tasks/TG-11-linguistic-robustness/T-069-evidence-pack-gap-register.md)), not a table: a gate added, renamed, re-thresholded or removed without the pack moving is a test failure.
- **Two fixture layers, kept distinct on purpose.** Sweep fixtures are small and synthetic and prove the **gate works**; measurement fixtures are the sized fixtures the gates are actually scored on. A sweep pass is never quoted as a robustness result, and that sentence is carried into the rendered view rather than left in the design.
- **Proxy labels are contract text, not commentary.** "Stationary additive white noise" (not babble, not room noise) and "reduced-articulation proxy" (not dysarthria, not slurred speech) are declared in the T-063 `degradation:` block so the gate output, the run manifest and the evidence pack all read one string. A qualification that lives only in prose beside a table is the first thing a summary drops, so it is a **field** in the record and a **rule** in the rules file.
- **The slur class is a round-trip class, and it is named after the mechanism.** Reduced articulation is produced by TTS rate/tempo/quality perturbation plus additive noise through the piper→whisper round trip; piper cannot be made dysarthric, so the cells are a stress proxy and real slurred or dysarthric speech is **GAP-2** with the consented recordings and privacy basis it would need. The vocabulary is fixed now so no later document can upgrade the proxy.
- **Nothing in the group acquires data.** Beyond the existing synthetic banks and the shipped single Hindi voice, the group adds no recordings, no noise corpus and no TTS voice; the three dimensions that would need them are GAP-1/GAP-2/GAP-3, each with what it would take to close. The task group does not carry a procurement decision, and the design says so rather than implying the gaps are closed by construction.

## Open questions (carried into the design and the task files, not silently resolved)

- **OQ-1 — do `query` rows supervise a `time` span** for the relative-day family? Today they supervise none, so the requirement's own example ("slot भोलि") does not hold. Owned by T-063; the recommendation is yes, with the slot-F1 population consequence stated.
- **OQ-2 — which dialect slices the product may claim.** Owned by T-062; the design's five-value inventory is a hypothesis, not a finding.
- **OQ-3 — is Tier B ever promoted to a gate?** Only if T-061 measures the model already within 6 points on `O2`/`O4`; otherwise it stays a reported diagnostic indefinitely.
- **OQ-4 — does the augmentation justify a retrain?** A group-end decision from T-061's measured gap and T-064's supply numbers. No retrain is budgeted in this group.
- **OQ-5 — does the Terai slice need within-Nepali code-mixing as a new *register*** (Maithili/Bhojpuri function words), or does `code_switched` already cover it? T-062 rules; if a new register is genuinely required, the mixture arithmetic must be revisited explicitly rather than changed silently.
- **OQ-6 — the artifact named "v4" in the brief** does not exist at this base; T-061 names its artifact by digest and run id so a later export changes only which digest is measured.
- **OQ-7 (design) — the SNR ladder's 3 dB floor** is taken from the existing augmentation band, not from a product requirement. If deployment is louder than the band anticipates, the *floor* is what needs revisiting, and the answer is a data-acquisition question (GAP-1), not a threshold tweak.
- **OQ-9 (design) — does the project procure the GAP-1 noise corpus and the GAP-3 voices?** Both are procurement decisions with licence and cost consequences and are outside a task group's authority; a "no" leaves the corresponding gate permanently unwired and **permanently visible as a gap**, which is the outcome the design prefers over an unwired gate that reads green.
- **Pre-existing, reported not fixed:** `tasks/index.md`'s rework-note links to T-049/T-050 carry one `..` too many for that file's depth (`../TG-02-voice-interface/…` resolves to `plan-tasks/TG-02-voice-interface/…`, which does not exist). It predates this task and was left untouched to keep the diff minimal.
- **Not verified here, by design:** every gate threshold is *proposed* with its justification; its adequacy is settled by the measurement tasks (T-061, T-065's first run) and by T-067's falsification, not by this document. Likewise the gap register is written from the code as it stands (one Hindi voice, no noise-mixing step, no dialect rows) — if a later change closes one, the gap moves only when its gate is wired.

## T-062 — dialect / degradation / accent inventory review: decisions

**Full record:** [`specs/T-062-notes.md`](T-062-notes.md) (determination record + the native-speaker review pack).
**Structured banks:** [`specs/T-062-dialect-banks.yaml`](T-062-dialect-banks.yaml) — the `dialects:` / `style:` / `degradation:` blocks T-063 lands into `annotation_rules.yaml`. Summary of the decisions the group's other tasks inherit:

- **`dialect` value set narrowed to three: `standard`, `eastern`, `doteli`** — matching the shipped `DialectLabel` (`eastern | doteli | default`). `central` **dropped** (published "Central" = Jumli/Khasani, and the value collides with `standard`); `western` **dropped as a label** (the far-west variety is Doteli, ISO `dty` — a *distinct language*); `terai` **dropped** (the Terai's mother tongues are Maithili/Bhojpuri/Bajjika/Awadhi/Tharu/Urdu — different languages, not dialects of Nepali). Answers the Far-Western and Terai questions explicitly.
- **The lexical consequence over the closed banks is thin, and that is the finding.** METHODS and app names are dialect-invariant English loans; the attested region-exclusive material (eastern `-इछ` perfectives, Doteli past forms) sits in **third-person positions outside the supervised spans**; the rest is pan-regional **spelling drift**, which is filed in its own table so it can never be counted as dialect evidence.
- **`style` confirmed orthogonal to `register`.** `clipped` kept (it is a text operation and co-occurs with any register; `elder_fragmented` is an undefined, unpopulated register and must not carry elision). `honorific` kept as metadata, **not gated**. `mixed_code` **dropped** — a genuinely different phenomenon from `code_switched`, but with no data (answers OQ-5: **no new register, so the 60/25/15 arithmetic is unchanged**).
- **Claimable slices:** `standard` claimed today; `eastern`/`doteli` **conditionally claimable** — gated on the review pack's answers, ≥300 twin pairs (T-064) and the gate passing (T-065); the status flips to `claimed` in the rules in the same commit as the first authored bank. Everything else unclaimed, with a **forbidden-claim list** ("supports Eastern Nepali", "supports Terai Nepali", "supports Nepali dialects" etc.).
- **Degradation:** ladder {15,10,5,3} dB **kept** (anchor restated as the *STT model's* augmentation band, `tools/train/src/dataset.py:32-43`; mixing domain must be declared — OQ-7). Cells **reshaped**: rate is two-sided (slow = elder realism and usually *helps* ASR; fast = the degrading direction), `noise_scale`/`noise_w` are synthesis-quality knobs, and each cell moves one parameter. **Every level/cell carries a transcript-change precondition** — no transcript change ⇒ `no_effect`, dropped rather than counted as a pass. Piper's flag spelling is version-dependent and the installed version is unpinned → T-066 must pin it and fail loudly on an unsupported flag.
- **Naming contract fixed as fields** (T-069 renders them): articulation = "reduced-articulation proxy" (never dysarthria/slurred); noise = "stationary additive white noise" (never babble/room noise); round trip = "synthetic STT-error profile induced by a non-native (Hindi) TTS rendering" (never accent modelling/coverage); multi-voice = "speaker-variation proxy" (never regional accent).
- **GAP-3 is closer than the design assumed:** two licence-clean Nepali piper voices are named and licensed in-tree — `ne_NP-google-medium` (CC BY-SA 4.0, sha256 still empty in `tools/fetch-tts-voices.sh`) and `ne_NP-chitwan-medium` (CC0, already digest-pinned there). Closing GAP-3 therefore needs ≥2 (ideally 3) piper-CLI-format voices pinned by digest, **not** a research project — with the caveats that both are *standard* read speech (speaker variation, not regional accent) and that the espeak-GPL gate applies. A new **GAP-5** records Terai-accented Nepali (L2) and within-Nepali contact mixing as data the project does not have.
- **FR-005 boundary recorded:** this review covers the corpus/evaluation half only; the on-device personalisation half (enrolment, packs, the flow that would make the dialect label non-inert) is runtime work and is untouched.
- **Discipline:** no audio recorded, no real transcript collected, no consent-bearing data created; the pinned corpus, the rules and every Swift/JSON resource were read, never modified.
