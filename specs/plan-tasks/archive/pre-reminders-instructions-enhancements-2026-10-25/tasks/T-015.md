## Description
Add the consent-gated calendar channel for significant events. `SignificantMedicationEvent` (value) plus `writeSignificantEvent(_:) -> SignificantEventWriteResult` (written / skipped with reason — no consent, no account — never silent). The write is a one-off (2-component key grammar, `CalendarShareMapper.swift` :24–44) enqueued **without** `markOutbound`; the mapper composes the significant-event draft with the family audience — every family contact with an email — consistent with the M-1 amended disclosure (T-012, landed). **D7 bookkeeping:** one-off keys never enter `store.outboundKeys` (the stale-twin sweep at :553–583 iterates that set only), and the reconcile known-key snapshot (:703, prefix-only today) is narrowed to slot-shaped keys so a one-off can never be read as "removed" and tombstoned. Writes are write-once with no re-assert; family curation in Google is the documented follow-up. The consent gate reuses the shipped `canShare`; no new consent surface is introduced.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Significant events reach the family calendar under its consent gate

  Scenario: A consented household receives the event as a calendar entry
    Given a household that granted family calendar sharing
    When a significant medication event is written
    Then a one-off calendar entry is created with the significant-event copy
    And its audience is every family contact with an email, per the amended disclosure

  Scenario: The calendar channel respects its consent gate (edge)
    Given a household that has not granted family calendar sharing
    When a significant event occurs
    Then no event is written to the family calendar for that household
    And the result reports the skip honestly (never silence)

  Scenario: A reconcile pass never tombstones a written one-off (failure guard)
    Given a written significant-event one-off entry
    When the reconcile pass computes its known-key snapshot and sweeps
    Then the one-off key is not part of the snapshot
    And the one-off is not tombstoned
    And the one-off key is absent from outboundKeys
```
