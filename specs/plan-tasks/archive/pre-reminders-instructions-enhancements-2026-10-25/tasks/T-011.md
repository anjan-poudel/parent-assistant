## Description
Make the notifier honest. `APNsProvider.sendPush` (:225–226) stops printing success and returning true: it returns a typed `PushOutcome` with `.notDelivered("transport_unimplemented")` (the transport is still the stub; that fact becomes visible in data). `notifyAll` maps each contact's outcome to its `NotificationResult` (success or the concrete channel + error code `transport_unimplemented`), with partial delivery reported as partial — never as success. `emitEventAlerted` (:199) emits the honest outcome instead of the hardcoded `"success"`. Extend `buildPayload` (:153) additively with `v: 1` (D9) and optional `entry_id_hash` supplied only where design-l2 pins it (hash-only; no name, no dose, no title ever). `eventReminder` payload shape is unchanged apart from the additive version key. **Every existing stub-success assertion in `FamilyNotifierTests` flips with it** — the explicit list below.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Significant events reach caregivers with honest delivery status

  Scenario: Partial delivery is reported as partial
    Given two family contacts with different channels
    When a missed-medication notification succeeds for one contact and fails for the other
    Then the successful contact's result records success with its channel
    And the failed contact's result records the failure with its channel and error code
    And the overall outcome is reported as partial, not as success

  Scenario: A notification that could not be delivered is not reported as delivered
    Given the push transport is not implemented for a contact
    When a significant-event notification is attempted for that contact
    Then the outcome is recorded as not delivered
    And no surface describes the caregiver as notified

  Scenario: The wire envelope stays PII-free (guard)
    Given any significant-event payload
    When the payload keys are inspected
    Then the envelope carries aps, alert_type, timestamp and v only
    And entry_id_hash appears only where supplied, as a hash, with no name, dose or photo content
```
