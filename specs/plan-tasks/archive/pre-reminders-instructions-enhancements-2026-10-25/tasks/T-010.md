## Description
Implement `rearmForSlotClockChange()` (m-1): after the mapping changes, re-resolve every slot entry through C1 with the new mapping and re-create its pending reminders through the standard creation path (persist before arm; no fire-time resolution). A `SlotClockRearmReport` carries the per-entry effects honestly: rearmed count, kept count, `unresolvedSlots`, `persistenceFailed`. Guards: an entry with a finalize in flight (challenge/confirmation active) is kept, never re-armed; the pass is idempotent (a second pass with unchanged inputs replaces nothing); failures never silently vanish — they surface in the report for the settings leaf lines (T-020).

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Feature: A mapping change re-arms the schedule through the guarded pass

  Scenario: The family changes the household mapping
    Given the family sets the morning slot to 07:30 in the medication settings
    When the schedule is re-armed
    Then the morning entry's reminders resolve to 07:30
    And resolving the same entry and mapping again yields the same 07:30 result (deterministic)

  Scenario: An entry with a finalize in flight is kept (edge)
    Given a slot entry with an active confirmation challenge
    When the re-arm pass runs
    Then that entry is kept, not re-armed
    And its acknowledgement sequence continues untouched

  Scenario: An entry unresolved under the new mapping is reported (failure)
    Given a slot entry whose slot cannot be resolved under the new mapping
    When the re-arm pass runs
    Then the entry stays unarmed
    And the report names the condition in unresolvedSlots

  Scenario: A second pass replaces nothing (edge)
    Given a completed re-arm pass with unchanged inputs
    When the pass runs again
    Then it replaces nothing
    And the report reflects the no-op honestly
```
