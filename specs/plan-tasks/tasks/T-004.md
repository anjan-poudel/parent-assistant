## Description
Create the pure resolver that maps a spoken acknowledgement to exactly one pending candidate: `AckResolution` (`.resolved(entryId)` / `.ambiguous([candidates])` / `.nothingPending` / `.unmatched`) plus the closed vocabulary tables (slot words and meal words in ne and en; acknowledgement phrases reused from the shipped `CommandRouter` lists — one vocabulary, no second list). Resolution is name-first, then slot narrowing, over the candidate set handed in (scheduledAt, canonical name, then id as the fixed ordering for outputs); it is a pure function of (utterance, candidate set, canonical names) with no firing order as an input, no clock, no LLM, no I/O. The shipped correction-normalized containment semantics are reused, respecting the pinned Devanagari grapheme behaviour — the resolver matches with the same Character-cluster discipline and does not "fix" it.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Voice acknowledgement with medication and slot

  Scenario: The elder names the medication and the slot
    Given the morning slot has a pending reminder for "Amlodipine"
    When the elder says "I have taken Amlodipine for morning before meal"
    Then the utterance resolves to that reminder and its entry
    And the acknowledgement continues through the safety sequence (FR-MSR-009)

  Scenario: Several medications share the slot — the named one resolves
    Given the morning slot has pending reminders for "Amlodipine" and "Metformin"
    When the elder says "I have taken Metformin for morning"
    Then the utterance resolves to the Metformin entry
    And the resolution was driven by what the elder said, not by firing or pending order

  Scenario: A simple acknowledgement still works when unambiguous (edge)
    Given exactly one reminder is pending
    When the elder says "Taken"
    Then the acknowledgement resolves to that pending reminder
    And the elder was not required to name the medication

  Scenario: A simple acknowledgement with several sharing the slot is not guessed (failure)
    Given the morning slot has pending reminders for "Amlodipine" and "Metformin"
    When the elder says "Taken"
    Then no medication is chosen by firing order
    And the resolution is ambiguous and defers to the sanctioned clarification (FR-MSR-008)
    And nothing is recorded until the clarification resolves
```

## Implementation notes
- Pure: receives facts, returns a value; no coordinator access, no timers (the walk window lives in T-013; the config type is T-006's `AckClarificationConfig`).
- The candidate order handed in is the coordinator's canonical sort (T-013); the resolver may mention order only in its diagnostics, never as resolution input.
- Denial handling is not this component: the refusal-first guard runs upstream (T-014) and the resolver is never reached by a denial.
- No LLM symbols, no egress, no user-visible literals except the vocabulary tables (which are tokens, not copy).
