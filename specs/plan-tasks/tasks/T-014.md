## Description
Wire the acknowledgement path end to end. `handleMedicationAcknowledgement` (:5381) stops selecting the oldest pending reminder as its primary path: it resolves through C3 (T-004) over the coordinator's candidates (IF-7), with `.nothingPending` keeping the shipped `router.noPendingReminder` line, `.unmatched` an honest no-match line, and `.ambiguous` opening the walk (IF-8). The shipped `oldestPendingReminderEntryId()` (:12929) remains only as the fallback path should the resolver be unavailable (C12). Resolution outcomes emit the PII-free `medication_ack_resolved` / `_ambiguous` / `_unmatched` observations (entry id hash only). **R-4 placement, stated unambiguously:** the walk consult is a sibling immediately ahead of the `if coordinator?.isAwaitingConfirmation == true` block (:1049), so the walk's end flips the flag and the block is skipped, and the refusal falls through to the ladder (the denial arm at :2491) — never `if`-inside the block. The denial guard runs first, unchanged (`medicationDenialPhrases` :2425 used at :2464 and :2491 only); the generic ambiguous re-prompt (:1221–1224) is unreachable during a walk (N-4); `yes`/`no` inside a walk route to the walk, not to the challenge interpreter. FR-MSR-012: the new utterance shapes ride the shipped turn pipeline unchanged (same fast lane, no new listening gap).

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Acknowledgements resolve by what was said; walks own yes and no

  Scenario: Several medications share the slot — the named one resolves
    Given the morning slot has pending reminders for "Amlodipine" and "Metformin"
    When the elder says "I have taken Metformin for morning"
    Then the utterance resolves to the Metformin entry
    And the resolution was driven by what the elder said, not by firing or pending order

  Scenario: A simple acknowledgement with several sharing the slot is not guessed (failure)
    Given the morning slot has pending reminders for "Amlodipine" and "Metformin"
    When the elder says "Taken"
    Then no medication is chosen by firing order
    And the system asks for clarification using the sanctioned copy (FR-MSR-008)
    And nothing is recorded until the clarification resolves

  Scenario: होइन inside a walk never reaches the challenge handler (risk-5 guard)
    Given a clarification walk is open
    When the elder answers होइन
    Then the walk ends as denied
    And the refusal is handled by the refusal-first ladder
    And the confirmation challenge handler never sees the utterance
    And no acknowledgement is recorded

  Scenario: An acknowledgement inside a walk restarts resolution (risk-5 guard)
    Given a clarification walk is open
    When the elder speaks a full acknowledgement naming a medication and slot
    Then resolution restarts against the pending set
    And the utterance is not consumed as a yes or a no

  Scenario: "No" to a challenge is not an acknowledgement (edge)
    Given the confirmation challenge has been issued
    When the elder answers "No"
    Then the challenge records a denial
    And the safety sequence does not advance to recording
```
