## Origin
Owner-reported defect (via coordinator, 2026-10-15), recorded as amendment 7:
scanning a label reading "one tablet twice a day" captures no schedule. A
frequency phrase has no clock time, so the scan→slots conversion (the m-3
hour table {8→morning; 12/13→afternoon; 19/20/21→evening}) yields nothing
today; the fix groups the frequency into slot rows.
