# TG-12: Crux-Resolution Pipeline

> **Jira Epic:** Crux-Resolution Pipeline

## Description

The product goal is an intent engine that reads *through* bad STT, dialects and regional speaking styles to the **crux** of a request — Nepali, SOV, often spoken telegraphically by an elder. Two mechanisms carry that goal, and this group designs and builds both.

**1. Canonicalization layer.** A dialect-aware pre-intent normalizer that rewrites dialectal, orthographic and STT-corrupted variants onto canonical forms **before** tokenization and encoding, and returns the canonicalized transcript *plus variant provenance*. The machinery to extend already ships: `DialectIdentifier` + `DialectBiasProfile` + `Resources/DialectLexicon.json` + `DialectCentroids.json` bias STT decode today, but nothing canonicalizes what the decoder emits. The group adds a `DialectCanonicalizer`, data-driven lexical variant maps per dialect region, orthographic normalization rules (halanta/anusvara/diacritic variants, common STT mis-segmentations, clipped elder styles), a defined composition with `InputSanitiser` and `NepaliTextNormalizer`, and the feed into the encoder — and into the 4B on escalation.

**2. Cascade as default.** The cascade mechanism already exists in the shipped router: the encoder serves at `c ≥ 0.7`, the `0.4 ≤ c < 0.7` sub-band is owned by `bandChecked`, and abstention or failure escalates to the picker brain in the same turn. What does **not** exist is the policy that makes encoder-first-with-4B-fallback the *default* local-brain mode. This group designs that policy — the gating conditions to flip the default, the latency budget, and the failure modes — and implements the flip behind a kill switch.

**3. TG-10 is the glue.** The canonicalizer's variant tables and the cascade's confidence thresholds are *not* frozen at design time. Both are data-file artifacts, which is exactly what makes them eligible to ride the [TG-10](../../TG-10-continuous-learning-loop/index.md) loop: captured corrections become candidate table entries and candidate threshold deltas, and neither takes effect until it clears the eight T-038 gates, beats the incumbent, and passes the human publish gate. This group cross-references TG-10; it does not redesign it.

**Safety-first, stated as a hard constraint.** Canonicalization is **lossless for safety**: the emergency path and the keyword net read the **original** transcript first, exactly as they do today, and canonicalization is only ever the intent-model's input. A canonicalizer output can never gate, suppress or rewrite what the keyword net sees. This is not a stylistic preference — the shipped med-ack matcher documents the exact hazard: a containment match turns the refusal "नखाए" (did not eat) into the acknowledgement "खाए" (ate). Any normalization rule that could collapse a negated form onto its positive is a safety defect, and [T-074](T-074-variant-table-authoring.md)/[T-077](T-077-canonicalization-losslessness-safety-verification.md) are built to catch it.

**Evidence discipline — every claim is measured, or it is a declared gap.** This group is written so that it can return a **negative result with numbers**. Every canonicalizer rule is sourced either from frequency evidence in the pinned 8,000-row corpus or from an authored fixture with a cited example set; a rule with neither is not authored. Every losslessness claim carries an explicit gate, not a prose assurance. The cascade-default flip conditions are **numeric gates measured on a named revision**, never adjectives. The design doc's §14 evidence pack is the ledger: `claim → measurement → threshold → fixture id → corpus evidence`, with `UNMEASURED — gap` recorded wherever the data does not exist yet, alongside exactly what would produce it. If the canonicalizer buys **+0 points** on a dialect slice, the design must be able to say so, print the number, and let the flip condition fail on it.

**Reconciliation with TG-08, TG-10 and TG-11.** This group consumes their contracts and changes none of their labels, numbers or gates. The 12 intent labels, the 13 BIO tags, the logit orders and the eight existing gates are untouched; the canonicalizer sits **upstream** of the encoder's tokenizer and the cascade policy sits **around** its confidence, so neither adds a class, a head or a band. The 8,000-row pinned corpus and its revision tag (`sha256(corpus)[:8]`) do not move. `IntentRouter`, `CommandRouter`, the keyword safety net, `TranscriptSanityGuard` and `IntentCommandCache` are not modified by the canonicalizer — it composes with them at declared seams.

**Reconciliation with TG-11 — the dialect inventory is theirs, not ours.** TG-11's [T-062](../../TG-11-linguistic-robustness/T-062-dialect-inventory-review.md) has a Nepali speaker validate the dialect inventory and the per-dialect variant banks before any of them are authored. This group **consumes** that inventory rather than assuming it: the Eastern/Central/Western/Terai regions named in the brief are a starting taxonomy, and `docs/research-sections/accent-adaptation.md` records the taxonomy itself as an open question ("Which clusters actually ship (Doteli complex? Eastern? Madhesi?)"). [T-072](T-072-canonicalizer-rules-schema-design.md) and [T-074](T-074-variant-table-authoring.md) are gated on T-062 for that reason. TG-11 owns the `dialect × style` dataset axes and the two new harness gates; TG-12 owns the runtime normalizer. The two meet at the same variant banks and must not fork them.

**Boundary this group does not cross.** No taxonomy change. No new model head — canonicalization is a deterministic, data-driven rewrite, never a black box and never a learned component at runtime. No retraining: the encoder's weights are not touched by this group; canonicalization changes its *input*. No change to the band constants (`IntentRouter.Config.default`, `acceptThreshold 0.7` / `rephraseThreshold 0.4`). No change to the emergency path, the medication-acknowledgement path or the confirmation flow. No user-facing setting is added — the default flip is a shipped-default change gated by measurement, not a preference toggle, and no user data leaves the device (FR-007/NFR-015).

**The honest limit, stated up front.** A variant table is only as good as its coverage: it can canonicalize a variant it has **seen**, and it cannot canonicalize one it has not. Coverage is therefore measured against the pinned 8k corpus in [T-070](T-070-variant-coverage-measurement.md) *before* anything is authored, and reported per slice — never as a blanket claim. Second, canonicalization cannot repair information the decoder never produced: if WhisperKit emits the wrong word entirely, a variant map that "corrects" it is guessing, and a guessing canonicalizer is worse than none. Rules are therefore **variant→canonical only where the variant is attested**, and T-072 must specify the abstain-to-passthrough behaviour for unattested tokens rather than inventing a correction.

## Group effort estimate

- Optimistic (T-070/T-071 in parallel on separate tracks — measurement is CPU/dev-box work, cascade measurement is device work; design track ahead of the authoring track; one iOS dev + one ML/data engineer + one native-speaker reviewer): 16–26 days
- Realistic (native-speaker validation round-trips through T-062, the T-074 authoring landing mid-stream, T-075 held by T-072's schema review, T-078 held by the T-038 harness's review and by device availability for the p95 measurements): 24–40 days
- Entry gate: T-070 and T-071 start immediately. **T-071 cannot start before the encoder runtime exists** ([T-037-a](../../TG-08-nepali-intent-encoder/T-037-runtime-integration/T-037-a-ios.md)); if T-033 returned NO-GO there is no encoder artifact to measure, and T-071's second half — the 4B-direct baseline — is the only half that runs. T-072 waits on T-070's coverage numbers and on TG-11's T-062 inventory; T-074 waits on T-072. **T-076 cannot flip a default for a brain that is not integrated**, so it is gated on T-037-a landing.
- **The group never extends the critical path of TG-08.** It adds no task to `T-033 → T-034 → T-035 → T-036 → T-037 → T-038`. T-070 and the authoring half of T-074 run on the CPU/authoring side; T-071 and T-078 are device measurements over the T-038 harness as it exists.
- **The flip is evidence-gated, not schedule-gated.** [T-073](T-073-cascade-default-policy-design.md) proposes the flip conditions; [T-078](T-078-latency-residency-default-flip-verification.md) verifies them. If the conditions are not met, the honest outcome is **not to flip** and to record why — a default that ships on schedule but misses NFR-002 is a worse outcome than a default that waits.
