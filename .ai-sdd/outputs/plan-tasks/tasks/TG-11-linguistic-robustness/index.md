# TG-11: Linguistic Robustness

> **Jira Epic:** Linguistic Robustness

## Description

The T-034 corpus and the T-038 gates measure the encoder on one word order and one variety of Nepali: the unmarked SOV order the authoring templates emit, in standard written Devanagari or its romanised/code-switched cousins. Two requirements say that is not enough. The first is **order insensitivity where the semantics ride on content words** — "भोलिको मौसम कस्तो छ" must classify as the weather question with `भोलि` recognised as the time material, because content words carry the meaning and the tail ("कस्तो छ") is decoration; the same must hold when the tail is not final. The second is **disambiguation of accents, dialects and speaking styles** — Nepali is SOV, and the user contrasts it explicitly with English SVO: a speaker of Eastern or Terai Nepali, or an elder who speaks telegraphically, must not lose the intent the standard-form speaker gets.

This group answers both with machinery that already exists and is already guarded. It adds a deterministic **permutation scheme** over the pinned corpus (the content core stays and keeps its order; the grammatical tail is redistributed within stated limits drawn from a closed operator family), an orthogonal **`dialect` × `style` axis** for the annotation rules, an extension of the **noise pass** into a declared degradation instrument (additive noise at a controlled SNR, synthesis rate/tempo/quality cells, a multi-voice interface), and **five robustness gates** in the existing T-038 harness: order-invariance, dialect, additive-noise SNR ladder, clipped tail, and reduced articulation (slur proxy). Each is paired against the same row's unperturbed twin in the same run, and each ships with a fixture that proves it trips in isolation.

**Every claim maps to a measurement.** The group's closing artifact is an **evidence pack** ([T-069](T-069-evidence-pack-gap-register.md)): dimension → mechanism → gate → threshold + justification → fixture id → row counts → revision → failing fixture, with a **gap register** for what the current corpus cannot measure (real-world noise GAP-1, real slurred/elder speech GAP-2, Nepali voices GAP-3, dialect rows GAP-4). A dimension that cannot be measured is recorded as a gap with the data needed to close it — never rendered as a passing gate. The noise and slur dimensions are named for what they are: **stationary additive white noise** and a **synthetic stress proxy**, not real-world noise and not dysarthria.

**Reconciliation with TG-08.** The group consumes T-034's rules, T-035's contract, T-036's data build and T-038's harness, and changes none of their *labels or numbers*. The 12 intent labels, the 13 BIO tags, the logit orders and the eight existing gates are untouched; the five new gates are additive. The 189 hand rows and the 7,811 generated rows of `eval/golden_corpus.jsonl` stay **byte-identical** — every robustness row lives in a new, separately-tagged fixture file, so the corpus revision tag (`sha256(corpus)[:8]`) does not move and every recorded baseline stays comparable.

**Two R&D tasks start immediately.** [T-061](T-061-order-robustness-baseline.md) measures what the shipped artifact actually does on permuted order before anyone commits to an augmentation strategy, and [T-062](T-062-dialect-inventory-review.md) has a Nepali speaker validate the dialect inventory, the per-dialect variant banks, the degradation taxonomy and the SNR ladder before any of them are authored. Nothing is authored until both answer.

**Boundary this group does not cross.** No taxonomy change (the closed 12-label rule at `annotation_rules.yaml:9-11` holds — "weather-query" is `query`, and there is no 13th label). No new model head: `dialect` and `style` are dataset metadata, never logits. No runtime code and no user-facing setting, so NFR-023/NFR-024 are not engaged. No retraining: T-061 measures the pinned artifact by digest, and whether the augmentation justifies a retrain is a group-end decision, not an assumption. **No new data acquisition**: no recordings, no consent-bearing data, no licensed noise corpus, no commissioned TTS voice — those are GAP-1/GAP-2/GAP-3, named in the evidence pack with the data each would need, not worked around inside a task group's budget.

**The honest limit, stated up front.** The 117M-parameter student can absorb tail redistribution it has seen with mass, orthographic variants over words it already knows, clipped tails, and a bounded SNR ladder; it cannot absorb a second lexicon, cannot normalise a surface into a resolved value, and cannot be order-invariant by architecture. Dialect coverage claims are therefore per-slice and evidence-bound, never blanket. The piper round trip produces **STT-error robustness, not accent modelling** — the shipped voice is a Hindi voice chosen as nearest-to-Nepali and `synthesize` passes no rate or prosody parameter today; [T-066](T-066-accent-noise-pass-extension.md) adds deterministic parameters and labels the output honestly rather than overclaiming. Two further limits are recorded as gaps rather than dressed up: the noise the group can generate is **stationary additive white noise** inside the band the project already trains on (3–15 dB), not babble or room noise (GAP-1), and the slur dimension is a **synthetic rate/tempo/quality stress proxy**, not dysarthric speech (GAP-2).

## Group effort estimate

- Optimistic (T-061/T-062 in parallel, T-063 design track ahead of the authoring track, one ML engineer + one native-speaker reviewer, the degradation grid at its cheapest): 16–26 days
- Realistic (native-speaker validation round-trips, the T-063 rules amendment landing mid-stream, the T-066 round-trip passes queued behind the shared GPU host, T-065 held by the T-038 harness's review, T-067/T-068/T-069 re-runs): 24–38 days
- Entry gate: none hard — T-061 and T-062 start immediately; T-063 waits on both; T-064 waits on T-063's amendment being landed and reviewed, because authoring against an unamended rules file produces rows the validation refuses. T-065 is the join point (it needs both the authored fixtures and the degradation fixtures), so its start is the group's real serialisation point.
- **Supply is priced, not assumed.** T-066's degradation supply costs `rows × (levels + cells) × variants_per_utterance` round trips against the shared GPU host, and T-064's generators are CPU-side. The estimate above assumes the grid is run at the declared size; a reduced grid is a recorded decision with its resolution loss stated (T-066), and shrinking it silently would move a gate's resolution without moving its threshold.
- **The group never extends the critical path of TG-08.** It adds no task to `T-033 → T-034 → T-035 → T-036 → T-037 → T-038`; T-065 *extends* the existing harness rather than forking it, and the whole group runs on the CPU/authoring side (T-061's fixture scoring is a bounded forward pass over ≈800 short utterances on the pinned artifact).
- **Supply is not free.** T-064 must clear the unchanged floors (`corpus_floor: 8000`, `hard_floor_stt_noised: 0.55`, `per_action_floor`) without diluting the pinned rows. If order/dialect supply cannot clear them, that is a finding to report, not a reason to re-normalise the mixture.
