## Key risks

1. **HIGH — PAD/liveness detection BLOCKER (T-014-a, T-014-b):** THREAT-001 from security-design-review.md requires a PAD design note to be reviewed and approved before either VoiceBiometricAuth subtask can start. This is a CI gate.
2. **HIGH — LLM OOM on low-end Android (T-018-b):** Runtime RAM check required; decline load if < 2.5 GB available with user-visible notice.
3. **HIGH — Emergency silent TTS failure (T-012-a, T-012-b, T-026-a, T-026-b):** Platform-native TTS fallback required; blocking CI test failure.
4. **HIGH — Safety-critical services not isolated from LLM (T-024–T-028):** Build target isolation enforced; CI build test verifies no LLM import.
5. **HIGH — Signal Protocol prekey exhaustion (T-030):** Pre-generate 100 prekeys; auto-refresh when supply < 5.
6. **MEDIUM — iOS BGTaskScheduler budget exceeded (T-028-a):** Local notifications as primary; BGTask supplemental only.
7. **MEDIUM — openWakeWord model size in app binary (T-005-a, T-005-b):** Download-on-first-launch with fallback to manual activation.
8. **HIGH — Candidate model access and licence unverified (T-033):** `ai4bharat/IndicBERT-v3-270M` is gated on Hugging Face (contact-sharing agreement) and its licence is only indirectly indicated as MIT; `jhu-clsp/mmBERT-small`'s terms and the MASSIVE MiniLM checkpoint's provenance must be verified before any base is selected. No training on an unverified base — T-033 records licence/access evidence per candidate and marks unverifiable candidates NOT USABLE.
9. **HIGH — On-device export feasibility unproven (T-033, T-037-a, T-037-b):** ModernBERT-family encoders (RoPE, GLU, Flash Attention 2) have no proven CoreML / ONNX Runtime Mobile / LiteRT export path; this is the single largest technical risk, larger than headline accuracy. T-033's export spike is the kill gate; the fallback is the incumbent fine-tuned LLM local brain with no new artifact.
10. **HIGH — Taxonomy reconciliation is safety-critical (T-034):** the proposal's MASSIVE-derived intent list omits `emergency`, `abstain`, `ack_med`, `music` and `guide` — all first-class in schema v2 (`build_dataset.py` VALID_ACTIONS, spec §9.1). Training on a MASSIVE-derived taxonomy would silently drop the emergency intent; T-034 reconciles onto the existing taxonomy, never the reverse.
11. **HIGH — Slot resolution conflict (T-034, T-035):** the proposal's resolved-value output style ("time = tomorrow 09:00") conflicts with the code-disposes principle — `ContactResolver` / `MethodResolver` / `NepaliTimeParser` / `MedicationResolver` resolve slots in code inside confirm-before-execute. The encoder must emit token spans (BIO) for slot candidates only; the design maps spans onto `InterpretedCommand` as spoken.
12. **HIGH — Tokenizer fertility and latency budget (T-033, T-038):** both leading candidates use ~256K Gemma-family vocabularies; romanised Nepali and Nepali-English code-switching (15% of the mixture, spec §9.2) must be measured for tokens-per-word before any latency claim. Gate: interpret p50 ≤ 1.0 s / p95 ≤ 2.0 s on the oldest supported device.
13. **HIGH — Regression vs the Gemini baseline (T-038):** the spec §10 gate "no worse than −3 points on closed intents vs `GeminiCommandInterpreter`" exists in config.yaml (`max_gap_vs_gemini: 0.03`) but is not yet enforced by `eval_golden.py`; so are abstention precision ≥ 90% and calibration ±10 points. T-038 wires all three, with failing fixtures proving each can fail the run.
14. **HIGH — Emergency recall hard gate (T-036, T-038):** corpus recall must stay at 1.00 and adversarial near-miss recall at ≥ 0.98; a single corpus miss fails the run and blocks artifact publication and the default-brain switch. The keyword net remains the constitution-mandated backstop before any model.
15. **MEDIUM — SetFit cannot do token-level slot filling (T-035):** a sentence-embedding + linear head cannot produce slot spans; it is at most a slotless fallback and the design must say so explicitly.
16. **MEDIUM — Accuracy claims in the proposal are unverified and non-Nepali (T-033):** 82.34% (MASSIVE MiniLM), 91.1% (SetFit) and 97.3% (NyayaBench English) are not Nepali numbers and are not comparable to this project's §10 gates. Each candidate is measured on the project's own golden corpus with the existing harness; no such number is carried into the design as evidence.

30. **HIGH — The continuous-learning loop's egress boundary is the project's first device-data channel (T-053, T-054, T-056, T-059):** the shipped flywheel log is content-bearing by design — `IntentLogStore` holds slot values (contact names included) and states it "leaves only via the family's explicit export" (`IntentLogStore.swift:8-14`) — and the loop's hashed egress (hashes/signals leave; raw audio and raw text do not) is a second channel over the same record. 31. **HIGH — The promotion gate can publish a regression, or be bypassed (T-058, T-060):** the eight T-038 gates are absolute floors (`config.yaml:56-69`) and the harness exits non-zero on any failure (`eval_golden.py:821-838`, `:895-898`), but a candidate can clear every floor and still lose to the brain the user actually has. The incumbent comparison rides on the corpus-revision binding (`@<hash8>`), whose missing-baseline case is UNEVALUATED and must fail closed (`:742-763`, `:814-815`). A promotion gate wired as a second, parallel decision path — or one that reads "no baseline" as "pass" — would publish unmeasured brains, and the weekly cadence multiplies the opportunity. Controls: T-058 adds the comparison to the existing single refusal list in `run_encoder_pipeline.py:303-330`, `:556-559`; T-060 drives the blocking cases, including an all-gates-pass candidate that loses to the incumbent and a stale-baseline candidate, and asserts the artifact was never written.
32. **HIGH — The encoder may have learned position rather than content (T-061, T-063):** the intent head reads a single pooled `[CLS]` vector (`encoder_contract.yaml:59-61`) over absolute position embeddings, and the corpus carries no order variation beyond a handful of hand-authored shapes — so the order-insensitivity the product needs is an untested assumption, not a property. If T-061's Tier A operators collapse the model, augmentation cannot fix it and the finding is an architecture/retraining question. Controls: T-061 measures before anything is built and is explicitly required to be able to return a no-go; the design records that a collapse routes to a new design task with a retrain (§11), never to an in-place contract edit, and T-063 must not promote Tier B into training to hide it.
33. **HIGH — Order/dialect augmentation opens a leak channel the existing guard cannot see (T-064, T-068):** the leak guard is a normalized-utterance membership test (`build_dataset.py:88-134`; `pipeline_guards.golden_keys`), and a permuted or dialect row is by construction not normalize-equal to its parent, so it is invisible to it — a permuted row that goes through the noise pass is invisible twice. The same blind spot is already measured for the existing noised rows: **118 rows whose `clean_utterance` parent is a golden utterance (22 keys)** (`build_encoder_dataset.py:609-611`). Controls: a transitive `parent_leak` guard counted separately from the exactness counter, every fixture registered in one place, `--waive-leak` not waiving it, and T-068 constructing one-hop and two-hop adversarial rows that must be refused end to end — tested by observing the refusal, because `load_golden_keys` warns rather than fails on a misregistered path.
34. **MEDIUM — A new gate that cannot resolve its own threshold (T-065, T-067):** the inputs are paired, so the standard error depends on the discordant rate and the pair count: the 3-point order gate needs ≥800 pairs (half-width ≈2.2 points at π_d≈0.10) and at 400 pairs the half-width (≈3.2 points) is *wider* than the gate; the 5-point dialect gate needs ≥300 twin pairs per claimed slice (≈3.6 points). A gate that cannot resolve its threshold gets waived or chased. Controls: the fixture sizes are design parameters recorded in the design (§7.3), the paired SE and half-width are printed with every gate result, and T-067 fails a gate whose scored population falls below the minimums rather than reporting a gap over the survivors.
35. **MEDIUM — Dialect coverage is overclaimed from a five-value axis (T-062, T-065):** naming Eastern/Central/Western/Terai invites the reading that all are supported, when the gate can only prove non-collapse on slices that were actually authored. Controls: T-062 fixes the claimed-versus-unclaimed list with its rationale, the gate is per-slice and evidence-bound, unclaimed slices are never silently aggregated, and the design deliberately does **not** invent an absolute accuracy floor — the per-slice `side_effect_precision ≥ 0.97` and `emergency_recall == 1.00` clauses carry the safety weight instead.
36. **MEDIUM — The piper round trip is called accent coverage (T-066):** the shipped voice is the Hindi voice `hi_IN-pratham-medium` chosen as nearest-to-Nepali (`config.yaml` `stt_noise.tts_voice`) and `synthesize` passes only `--model`/`--output_file` (`stt_noise.py:30-48`), so the pass produces an STT error profile, not accent modelling — it cannot be made to speak Eastern Nepali. Controls: T-062 rules on whether the approximation may be called accent-robustness at all; T-066 applies that ruling verbatim, labels the output in the run manifest, and forbids inferring a dialect or style tag from the synthesis; the design states the limit rather than dressing it up (§5.5).
37. **MEDIUM — A dialect row the resolver cannot consume (T-064):** the encoder emits surfaces and code disposes of them (`encoder_contract.yaml:136-145`, `:217`, `:226-231`), so training it to recognise a surface the next layer rejects converts a recognition failure into a silent resolution failure inside confirm-before-execute. Control: a resolve-through check against the shipped resolvers' stated tolerance on every span-carrying dialect row, with `resolver_blocked` marking and reporting, and blocked rows excluded from the gate's shippable claim.
38. **MEDIUM — The pinned corpus or its revision tag moves (T-064, T-068):** appending rows would move `sha256(corpus)[:8]` (`eval_golden.py:606`) and turn every recorded baseline UNEVALUATED through `read_gemini_baseline`, which fails closed (`:505-537`, `:749-759`, `:814-815`). Controls: all robustness rows live in new, separately-tagged fixture files; T-068 records the pre-TG-11 digest before the work, checks the 189 hand rows byte-for-byte separately from the whole file, and proves the tag binding still bites in both directions.
39. **LOW/MEDIUM — The `query` span-policy question is settled by accident (T-063):** `query` rows supervise no spans today, so the requirement's own example ("भोलिको मौसम कस्तो छ" with `भोलि` as a slot) does not hold under the T-034 policy — a robustness task must not smuggle a span-policy change in as an augmentation. Control: recorded as design OQ-1, owned by T-063, with its consequences stated (the harness's slot-F1 population changes; no pinned row is re-annotated).
40. **HIGH — A dimension is measured under a proxy and then reported as the thing itself (T-066, T-069):** the only noise the project can generate is stationary additive white noise inside the band it already trains on (`dataset.py:42-43`) — not babble, a television or a market — and the articulation grid is a synthetic rate/tempo/quality stress test, not slurred or dysarthric speech. A summary that drops the qualifier turns a proxy into a coverage claim, and nothing in a results table forces a reader to keep it. Controls: GAP-1 and GAP-2 record what is unmeasurable and the data that would close it, the permitted label for each class is declared as contract text in T-063's `degradation:` block, T-066 labels the output in the run manifest, and T-069 carries the qualification as a field of the record and fails a gate whose justification is empty.
41. **HIGH — An unwired dimension is rendered green (T-065, T-069):** the accent gate has no fixture because the voice bank holds one Hindi voice (`config.yaml:23`; no `*.onnx` in the tree), and the two natural ways to complete a table are to fill the cell with a pass or to leave the row out — both of which read as "measured". Controls: `accent_voices` is declared in `config.yaml` but omitted from the `gates` dict, the manifest reports GAP-3 with its data requirement, T-067 proves no prediction vector, fixture or code path can emit it as passing, and T-069 fails on a gap removed without a wired gate.
42. **MEDIUM — The evidence pack drifts from the harness (T-069):** a table of thresholds and fixture ids maintained by hand disagrees with the code the first time either moves, and it disagrees in the direction that looks fine — the documents stay green while the harness changed. Controls: every field is derived (thresholds read from `config.yaml`, counts from the run manifest, hashes from the fixtures), a gate added, renamed, re-thresholded or removed without the pack moving is a test failure, and the rendered view is generated rather than edited.
43. **MEDIUM — The degradation supply cost is discovered inside the run (T-066):** the passes cost `rows × (levels + cells) × variants_per_utterance` round trips against a shared GPU host, and the cheapest way to fit a budget is to shrink the grid silently — which moves a gate's resolution without moving its threshold, so the gate still reads green against a smaller measurement than its arithmetic assumed. Controls: the cost is stated before the run, a reduced grid is a recorded decision with its resolution loss stated, and a level or cell declared in config but absent from the fixture is reported as missing rather than skipped (T-065).
32. **HIGH — The cascade's worst-case turn is 12 s against NFR-002's 4 s (T-073, T-076, T-078):** the encoder's timeout is 2.0 s with zero retries (`IntentEncoderInterpreter.swift:168-169`), the picker brain's is 10 s with one retry (`LocalIntentInterpreter.swift:47-49`), and `LocalBrainChain` passes no remaining-time budget to its stand-in — so an escalated turn can pay both sequentially, in the mode where the user has already waited for one model to fail. The cascade is built and shipped `cascade: nil` (default OFF), which is the only reason this has never been observed; making encoder-first the default would expose it. Controls: T-073 makes a turn-level deadline a **precondition** of the flip rather than a follow-up, T-076 implements it in the chain (not by lowering the picker's timeout, which serves the single-brain default too), and T-078 verifies it adversarially by forcing an encoder timeout. The flip does not land while this is unverified.
33. **HIGH (SAFETY) — A canonicalization rule can collapse a negated form onto its positive (T-074, T-077):** the shipped med-ack matcher matches whole tokens precisely because `खाए` sits inside `नखाए` (`CommandRouter.swift:1443-1452`, *"a containment match turns a refusal into a medication acknowledgement"*). A variant table built to "repair" dialectal or orthographic forms can produce exactly that collapse, and the failure is a wrong medication acknowledgement rather than a missed one. Controls: the frozen safety set (17 emergency phrases at `:1468-1473`, med-ack and denial tokens at `:1508-1534`, the negation class), a `negationMarkerTouched` validator that refuses the **whole table** at authoring time, and T-077's two-clause equality gate computed by calling the net's **own** matchers — plus a required negative arm (`नखाए` → `खाए`) that must be observed failing the gate before the passing result is trusted. The keyword net keeps reading the **original** transcript; canonicalization is only ever the model's input (FR-009).
34. **MEDIUM — Canonicalization may buy nothing, and the group must be able to say so (T-070, T-078):** the honest prior is that a 117M-parameter student trained on the standard forms may already generalise to some attested variants, and that the corpus's attested variant surface over 8 000 rows is small. A group that cannot report "+0 on slice X" will be read as having improved something. Controls: every rule is sourced from corpus frequency evidence or a cited authored fixture or it is not authored; the effect is measured per dialect slice with zeroes and negatives printed (T-078); and the flip conditions are numeric gates on a named revision, so a slice that gains nothing can fail one.
35. **MEDIUM — The canonicalizer's dialect axis is inert in production today (T-072, T-074):** the tables are selected by a label read from `DialectPreference`, whose only writer (`WhisperKitSpeechRecognizer.applyDialectLabel(_:)`) has **no production callers** — the embedding/classification bridge beside it is documented as *"the future enrolment flow"* — so the persisted label is `.default` in the field, and the shipped `SEED-CENTROIDS` table could not clear its own 0.6 gate anyway (a test asserts *"A seed table must never classify anything"*). Consequence: the per-dialect tables are inert until an enrolment flow and a calibrated centroid table land, and the orthographic/pan-regional half is the half that can act now. Recorded rather than worked around — the group neither builds an enrolment flow (TG-02/TG-09 territory) nor populates the centroid table (design §10, out of scope).
32. **HIGH — The environment benchmark can be invalid while looking green (T-080, T-081, T-082):** three independent ways to produce a number that looks like evidence and is not. (a) **Licence** — a noise or RIR corpus used without checking what its licence permits, or a reverberant-speech mix whose base speech corpus is research-only, makes the fixture supply unusable after the numbers are published; the check is per source and per use (measurement input vs committed derivative), and `NOT USABLE` is the verdict for an unverifiable licence, following T-033's base-model discipline (`plan.md` Risk 8). (b) **Transport validity** — convolution is not a room: a cell rendered with one two-second noise excerpt reused across 800 utterances, an SNR computed over the whole file instead of the speech-active region, or an undeclared mixing domain produces a number that is about the fixture rather than the environment. (c) **Statistical power** — a 5-point band scored on 100 paired rows has a 6.2-point CI half-width and cannot fail honestly; ~46 cells at a 95% per-cell level will show ~2 spurious failures by chance, so a per-cell result reported without the pooled corrected verdict (or vice versa) is misleading on its own. Controls: T-080's ledger with verbatim licence lines and digests, T-081's declared bands and sample-size arithmetic printed per cell, T-082's digest-pinned deterministic rendering with achieved-SNR and mixing-domain recorded, and T-084's five cell states plus the pooled/per-cell reporting rule.
33. **HIGH — A gate that has never failed is an untested gate (T-083, T-084, T-087):** the environment gates are new, and new gates fail in two directions — they never trip (so a broken metric passes everything) or they trip for the wrong reason (so a fixture proves nothing). The specific hazard here is pooling: safety gates (`emergency_recall = 1.00`, `side_effect_precision >= 0.97`, `abstention_precision >= 0.90`) evaluated across all cells hide a condition that breaks safety in exactly one, which is the condition the benchmark exists to find. Controls: T-087's one failing fixture per gate each exiting with *exactly* its target gate (`eval/fixtures/run_fixture_sweep.sh:1-9` is the pattern), the pooled-vs-per-cell negative control, the unbound-reference control that must fail closed (`gemini_gap_unevaluated`, `eval_golden.py:814-815`), the leak fixture whose expected outcome is refusal, and T-085's structural-tier tests. A gate without a fixture is reported UNPROVEN and blocks the group.
